# Tools

Browser-based finance and accounting utilities maintained for GSS staff, the
Accountant, and the Financial Officer. Both tools run locally inside your browser
tab and never upload spreadsheets or organization payment details to an external
server. Shortcuts to both pages also appear in the
[Executives & Staff Guide](https://ubcgss.github.io/executives_staff_guide/docs/05-financial-officer/tools/).

| Tool | What it's for | Primary users |
|------|---------------|---------------|
| [Budget Check]({% link docs/tools/budget-check.md %}) | Lists likely errors in the master budget: unsourced parameters, rolled-forward lines, hard-coded numbers, totals that don't add up, and missing required costs | Accountant, GM, FO |
| [External Fund Transfer Fees]({% link docs/tools/external-fund-transfer-fees.md %}) | Calculates policy administration fees and net transfer value for one participating-organization batch, saves a local PDF record, and documents the Zoho Books `2230` / `4800` entries | GM, Accountant, FO |
